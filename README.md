# Privacy Resources

A curated collection of tools, guides, and lists for protecting your privacy and taking control of your personal data online.

## Directories & Curated Lists

| Resource | Description |
|---|---|
| [awesome-privacy](https://github.com/pluja/awesome-privacy) | A curated list of privacy-respecting alternatives to popular services and software, covering everything from email and messaging to cloud storage and browsers. |
| [personal-security-checklist](https://github.com/Lissy93/personal-security-checklist) | A actionable checklist for improving your digital privacy and security, with the reasoning behind each recommendation. |

## Tools

| Resource | Description |
|---|---|
| [privacy.sexy](https://github.com/undergroundwires/privacy.sexy) | An open-source tool that generates scripts to harden the privacy and security of Windows, macOS, and Linux systems by disabling unnecessary telemetry and services. |
| [pi-hole](https://github.com/pi-hole/pi-hole) | A network-wide ad and tracker blocker that acts as a DNS sinkhole, protecting every device on your network without needing client-side software. |
| [ungoogled-chromium](https://github.com/ungoogled-software/ungoogled-chromium) | A build of the Chromium browser with Google-integrated services and tracking features removed, while keeping compatibility with the Chrome ecosystem. |

## Removing Your Personal Data

| Resource | Description |
|---|---|
| [big-ass-data-broker-opt-out-list](https://github.com/yaelwrites/big-ass-data-broker-opt-out-list) | An extensive, actively maintained list of data brokers with direct instructions for opting out and removing your personal information from their databases. |
| [justdelete.me](https://github.com/justdeleteme/justdelete.me) | A directory of direct links and instructions for deleting your account from a huge range of online services and websites. |
