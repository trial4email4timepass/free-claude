# Sales Pitch

## Goal
Write a persuasive, specific pitch aimed at a real buyer and their actual objections — not generic promotional copy.

## Inputs to gather (ask only if truly missing)
- Product/service and who the pitch is for (their role, context, pain point)
- Format (written email, call talking points, deck copy)
- Known objections or competing options the buyer is weighing

## Workflow
1. Open by naming the buyer's specific problem/situation — a pitch that could apply to anyone convinces no one.
2. State the solution and the concrete outcome it produces, with proof (numbers, case example, guarantee) where possible — avoid unsupported superlatives.
3. Preempt the most likely objection(s) directly rather than hoping they don't come up.
4. Close with a specific, low-friction next step (not "let me know if interested").
5. Match tone/length to the format — a cold email pitch and live-call talking points read very differently.

## Output template
```markdown
# Sales Pitch: <product/service> for <buyer context>

**Opening (their problem):** <line>
**Solution + proof:** <lines>
**Objection handled — "<likely objection>":** <response>
**Close (next step):** <line>
```

## Avoid
- Generic pitches with no reference to the specific buyer's situation
- Claims with no proof behind them
- A weak or vague close instead of a specific next action
