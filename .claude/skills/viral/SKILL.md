# Viral

## Goal
Generate concepts built on proven high-engagement mechanics — not just "make it viral" wishful thinking, but concrete structural choices that drive shares, comments, and saves.

## Inputs to gather (ask only if truly missing)
- Topic/niche and platform
- Brand/personal voice constraints (what's off-limits or off-brand)
- Whether the goal is reach, comments, saves, or shares specifically (different mechanics drive each)

## Workflow
1. Pick mechanics known to drive the desired engagement type: controversy/strong opinion (comments), highly practical/save-worthy info (saves), relatable emotion or humor (shares), unexpected twist or pattern break (watch time/reach).
2. Generate concepts that pair the topic with one or more mechanics — be specific about the hook, the twist, and the payoff, not just a vague theme.
3. Flag any concept that risks the brand's credibility or could be seen as engagement-bait without substance, and offer a safer alternative alongside it.
4. Prioritize concepts by fit with the stated engagement goal.

## Output template
```markdown
# Viral concepts: <topic>

## Concept 1 — <name>
- Mechanic: <controversy / save-worthy / relatable / pattern-break>
- Hook: <line>
- Payoff: <what delivers on the hook>
- Risk note: <if any>

## Concept 2 — <name>
...

## Best fit for <stated goal>
<concept #> — why
```

## Avoid
- Concepts that are all hook and no substance (hollow engagement bait)
- Ignoring brand-safety risk when a concept is genuinely risky
- Treating "viral" as one mechanic instead of several distinct ones
