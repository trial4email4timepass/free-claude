# Lead Generation

## Goal
Produce a concrete lead-gen strategy focused on qualified leads — not just traffic or vanity signups.

## Inputs to gather (ask only if truly missing)
- Product/service and target audience
- What counts as a "qualified" lead here (criteria)
- Existing channels/assets (website, email list, sales team) and budget level

## Workflow
1. Define lead qualification criteria clearly — this determines what "success" means for every tactic below.
2. Choose lead-gen channels/tactics that fit where the audience already is and the budget level (e.g. content + SEO, outbound, paid ads, partnerships, referrals) — justify each choice.
3. For each tactic, specify the offer/hook that gets someone to convert (lead magnet, demo, free trial, consult) — a channel without a compelling offer won't generate leads.
4. Define the qualification/nurture step after capture — raw form-fills aren't qualified leads until scored or nurtured.
5. Note how each tactic will be measured (cost per qualified lead, conversion rate).

## Output template
```markdown
# Lead Generation Strategy: <product/service>

**Qualified lead definition:** <criteria>

## Tactic 1 — <name>
Offer/hook: <what gets them to convert>
Qualification step: <how raw leads get scored/nurtured>
Metric: <cost per qualified lead / conversion rate>

## Tactic 2 — <name>
...
```

## Avoid
- Tactics aimed at raw volume with no qualification step
- No stated offer — a channel plan without a lead magnet/hook rarely converts
- Ignoring budget/resource constraints when choosing tactics
